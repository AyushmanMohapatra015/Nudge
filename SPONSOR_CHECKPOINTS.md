## Repeat before activation

1. Confirm the actual account plan and balance; a published sponsor offer is not a credited account.
2. Check expiry against **submission and judging**, plus service-specific eligible usage and model/licence coverage. Do not assume the judging dates from the submission deadline.
3. Record accrued charges/usage as well as remaining credits. For Render, credit balance changes at billing, not continuously; inspect the current usage estimate too.
4. Identify what happens at exhaustion: suspension, rejection, renewal or payment-method charging. Alerts/estimated usage are not a hard spending cap.
5. Review a concrete configuration and maximum approved batch/service duration before activation. Keys stay local/private; account summaries shared here contain no personal identifiers.
6. At each later integration step, remind the owner of the relevant signup/usage/expiry checkpoint. No automatic signup, deployment, upgrade or real allowance approval follows from the roadmap.

The current research, code tests and documented local judge setup continue while an external checkpoint is pending. Source/privacy review and controlled judge access are required before exposing a credentialed owner workspace. Official sources and resource-specific fit remain in SPONSOR_RESOURCES.md, RENDER_SIGNUP.md and RENDER_DEPLOYMENT.md.
