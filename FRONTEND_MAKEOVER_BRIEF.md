## Motion and asset requirements

Motion should connect information and provide feedback. Prefer transform/opacity for ordinary transitions; provide reduced-motion behavior and keep controls usable without animation. Heavy video/frame/WebGL assets need a measured mobile loading/memory budget, deferred loading where appropriate, a useful static fallback and clear asset rights. Native scrolling, keyboard focus, readable money/status text and decision controls must remain available.

The owner supplied a tutorial containing hero videos, frame sequences, bento sections, carousels and 3D objects. Each is optional. Include only elements that make Nudge's specific problem and workflow easier to understand. A new tool or asset must not require unapproved spending or redistribute restricted template content into the public MIT repository.

Primary guidance consulted October26: [web.dev animation performance](https://web.dev/articles/animations-and-performance), [video performance](https://web.dev/learn/performance/video-performance), [Motion reduced-motion behavior](https://motion.dev/docs/react-use-reduced-motion). This is design research, not a compatibility, performance or licensing test of an installed animation package.
